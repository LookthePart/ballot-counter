Privacy & Safety

  

    Your data stays on this phone
    
    
      
  • Every number, note and report is saved only on this phone, inside this app.
    • 
      
  • Nothing is ever sent to a server, a shared database, or anyone else. There are no accounts, logins, ads or tracking.
    • 
      
  • It works with no internet or cell signal. Internet is only needed the very first time you open it, to put it on your phone.
    • 
      
  • Data only leaves the phone if you choose it: when you tap Copy Report, Backup App, Export to Spreadsheet or Download Report Images, and then pick where to send it.
    • 
      
  • You can erase everything at any time with Delete all data from application on the Download page.
    • 
    

  

  

    It is a calculator, nothing more
    
    
      
  • It does not connect to voting machines, poll books, touchpads or any election system.
    • 
      
  • It does not count, change or store ballots or voter information. It only does arithmetic on the numbers you type in.
    • 
      
  • It does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.
    • 
      
  • It is not an official election tool and is not connected to any election office. Always follow your official procedures and forms. This app is just a way to double-check the math.
    • 
    

  


  
Definitions

  

    
ePoll
Number read from the touchpad check-in machine. Represents number of voter check-ins.

    
Public Count
Number read from public count on machine.

    
Protected Count
Number read from protected count on machine.

    
Ballots Received
Total number of ballots received at the polling location that day. Includes both Large Ballots and Small Cards.

    
Sealed Ballots
Ballots that remained in original packaging. Never opened.

    
Unsealed Ballots
Ballots that were removed from their packaging but never used.

  

  

    Calculation Definitions
    
Lifetime
Cumulative total from Day 1 of Election Cycle

    
Lifetime Change
Total change since Day 1 of Election Cycle

    
Change from Yesterday
Difference in value from yesterday to today. A way to look at daily changes in a running total throughout Election Cycle.
